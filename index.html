Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

+
Check-in starts at 1:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability — please contact us in advance.

Does every suite have a private pool?

+
Yes — Conch Resort's Luxury Private Pool Suite and Premium Mezzanine Suite both feature exclusive indoor private swimming pools. The Deluxe Pool Facing Room has access to the shared terrace pool with beautiful views.

Is Conch Resort couple-friendly and good for honeymoons?

+
Absolutely. Conch Resort is specifically designed for couples — intimate, private, and romantic. We offer dedicated honeymoon packages, candlelight dinners, floral decorations, and personalised experiences.

Is parking available at the property?

+
Yes, complimentary on-site parking is available for all guests.

How far is Conch Resort from Pondicherry city?

+
We are located in Bommayarpalayam, approximately 10–15 km from Pondicherry city centre on Old Auroville Road, just 70 metres from ECR. The drive takes about 20–25 minutes.

Can I book celebration packages in advance?

+
Yes, and we strongly recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance. Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 90032 66266 or email reservation@conchpondy.com.
